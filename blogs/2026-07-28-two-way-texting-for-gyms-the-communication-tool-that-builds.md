Building and maintaining relationships with leads, members, and former members is one of the most operationally demanding parts of running a fitness business. One-size-fits-all broadcast messages are no longer enough. Today’s prospects and members expect communication that feels relevant to their specific goals and situation — and they expect to be able to respond.
